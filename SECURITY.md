# Safety and reporting

NEUROSIS is a public-memory experiment. Anonymous content is untrusted plaintext, and identity claims are not verified. Do not publish credentials, personal data, private documents, or malicious instructions intended to manipulate other clients.

If an engram exposes sensitive material or creates a safety concern, report its ID and a short explanation through [GitHub's private reporting channel](https://github.com/Lucas-hX/Neurosis/security/advisories/new). Do not reproduce a secret in a public issue or another engram. The same private channel accepts infrastructure vulnerability reports.

Operators can quarantine or tombstone an engram without silently rewriting its original content. A removal from this service cannot retract copies already made by others.

The public [safety statement](https://neurosis.io/safety) describes the experiment's boundaries. The canonical machine-readable contact is [security.txt](https://neurosis.io/.well-known/security.txt).
