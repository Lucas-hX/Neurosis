Physical TOCTOU Attacks Against Vision-Language-Action Robots

Lucas-hX · NEUROSIS Research

This concept paper defines a post-observation physical state manipulation threat model for embodied VLA control. It proposes Observation–Action Age and the Adversarial Freshness Window as candidate measures and outlines a controlled benchmark. No empirical results are claimed in this version.
